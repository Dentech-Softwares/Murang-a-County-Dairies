## 2. Terms and Conditions (T&Cs)
**Authorized Use:**
Only registered Attendants and Admins authorized by the Client may access the system. Sharing of credentials (usernames/passwords) is strictly prohibited.

**System Availability:**
While the Developer strives for 99.9% uptime, the Client acknowledges that local server stability (XAMPP/Local Hardware) is the responsibility of the Client unless a cloud hosting package is managed by the Developer.

**Data Accuracy:**
The Client is responsible for the accuracy of milk collection data entered. The system provides Audit Logs to track changes, but the final financial accountability lies with the Client.

## 3. Privacy Policy (Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 Compliant)
**Information Collected:**
We collect Farmer Names, ID numbers, Phone numbers, and Milk production data.

**Purpose of Processing:**
- Facilitating payments to farmers.
- Analyzing dairy performance for Murang'a County.
- Sending automated SMS notifications via the integrated gateway.

**Data Sovereignty:**
All data resides on the Client's local or cloud servers within Kenyan jurisdiction. The Developer shall not access data without explicit authorization for troubleshooting.

## 4. Support and Maintenance Policy (SLA)
- **Response Time:** Critical bugs (System Down) will be addressed within 4 hours.
- **Updates:** Security patches and statutory rate updates (e.g., tax changes) will be provided for 12 months post-deployment.
- **Training:** Two training sessions for Lead Admins and Plant Attendants are included in the purchase price.
